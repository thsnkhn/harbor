## New

- Set a preferred video format and maximum resolution in Media settings. Choose MP4 compatibility for H.264 video and AAC audio when available.
- Add multiple media URLs at once. Harbor checks each URL and keeps failed entries available for correction.
- Open Preview from the torrent inspector to change file selection after a download starts. Completed files show as downloaded.
- Use Clear Completed or Command-Delete to remove download entries and keep their files.
- Optionally remove completed entries automatically. Torrents remain listed until seeding stops.
- Set Harbor as the default app for torrent files and magnet links from Torrent settings.

## Improvements

- Show share-ratio controls when seeding after download is enabled. Torrents can still upload while downloading.
- Confirm moving download data to Trash with Return, or cancel with Escape.
- Improve browser download handling for sites that require a browser session.
- Reduce torrent network activity while Harbor is idle.
- Prevent completed torrents from returning after automatic cleanup by moving their matching source files from the watch folder to Trash.
